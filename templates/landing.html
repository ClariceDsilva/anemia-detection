Understanding Anemia

What is anemia, and why does it matter?

Anemia occurs when the blood doesn't have enough healthy red blood cells, or enough hemoglobin, to carry adequate oxygen to the body's tissues. Hemoglobin is the protein inside red blood cells that binds oxygen in the lungs and delivers it throughout the body — when levels drop, tissues and organs receive less oxygen than they need.

😴

Fatigue & Weakness

Persistent tiredness, even after rest, is one of the earliest and most common signs.

🎨

Pale Skin, Nails & Gums

Reduced hemoglobin can visibly change the color of skin, nail beds, and inner eyelids.

💨

Shortness of Breath

Lower oxygen delivery can make everyday activity feel more strenuous than usual.

💫

Dizziness

Lightheadedness or trouble concentrating can occur when the brain receives less oxygen.

🧊

Cold Hands & Feet

Poor circulation of oxygen-rich blood can leave extremities feeling cold.

❤️

Irregular Heartbeat

In more pronounced cases, the heart may work harder to compensate, affecting rhythm.

Anemia is common worldwide and often goes unnoticed because early symptoms can be subtle or mistaken for everyday tiredness. A simple visual screening step — like checking the color of the palms or nail beds — can help someone decide whether it's worth pursuing a proper blood test, without replacing one.

The Process

How it works

Three steps, from image to estimate.

01 — Upload

Upload an image

Provide a clear photo of a palm or nail bed through the AnemiaAI tool.

02 — Analyze

The pipeline processes it

The image runs through the project's computer-vision and machine-learning pipeline, which extracts color and texture features.

03 — Estimate

Get a risk estimate

The system returns an anemia-risk estimate to help you decide whether further testing is worthwhile.
